### Glass and Gold-Leaf Mosaic

- **Creator:** [Chris Riley @LCSlates](https://x.com/LCSlates)
- **Watch:** [Watch video](https://x.com/LCSlates/status/2102503027340988559)
- **Original post:** [Creator's post](https://x.com/LCSlates/status/2102503027340988559)
- **About:** Loose tiles of a gold-leaf mosaic rise, flip, and reassemble into fish, birds, and stars as the scene cycles from night to day.
- **Implementation:** The published prompt asks for one HTML file in WebGL2 and plain JavaScript, without libraries or image, font, or audio files. An [agent process note](https://x.com/LCSlates/status/2102503030469833168) describes browser-synthesized sound and frame-by-frame revisions.
- **Prompt:** A [prompt excerpt](https://x.com/LCSlates/status/2102503028859211905) is public, but the reply cuts off mid-sentence.

> Make an 80 second square animated film as a single HTML file, using WebGL2 and plain JavaScript, with no libraries and no image, font or audio files. It should look like a glass and gold leaf wall mosaic whose tiles were never glued down, so they can lift, flip, fly and click back into place.
