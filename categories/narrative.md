### Claude 的一生

- **作者**：[@shfred0](https://x.com/shfred0)
- **样片**：[观看视频](https://x.com/shfred0/status/2102495989194236158)

- **提示词**：投稿者提供，公开出处待核对。

> Based on what you have done already in this session, I want you to create a full two-minute episode. I want you to generate yourself again and really try to express yourself as you wish. Make your feelings come out, give yourself a voice, and use a language of your choice. Create sound effects as well as a soundtrack that perfectly fits and matches the episode.
>
> I will only give you two words that MUST be included in the title, everything else is your choice.
>
> Words to use in the title : “Be”, “Human”.

注：这段提示词依赖同一会话之前的内容（“what you have done already in this session”），单独使用可能无法复现原作。
