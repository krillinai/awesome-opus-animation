An open collection of animations, interactive scenes, and playable projects made with the help of AI models. Starting with Claude Opus 5.5, it spans narrative shorts, hand-drawn music videos, code animation, 3D worlds, explainers, and games. Other models are welcome too.

This collection documents **both the work and how it was made**. Each entry links back to its creator and the original post, with a watchable sample and, when available, the model's role, the creator's prompt, production time, and a live demo. We do not infer undisclosed details from the finished video.

## How to Use

- **Explore:** Browse featured previews below; open a category to see every entry.
- **Watch and learn:** Click a thumbnail for the creator's post, or a title for the case details and public production notes.
- **Suggest a case:** Share the creator's X post in an [issue](https://github.com/krillinai/awesome-opus-animation/issues/new), or follow the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) to open a pull request.

## Curation Guidelines

- Prefer the creator's original X post. Credit the creator and link to a watchable sample; a repost does not replace the original source.
- Record the model and version. Distinguish **code written by a model**, **a model assisting production**, and **footage generated by a video model**. Do not list a case whose model involvement cannot be verified.
- Quote prompts as published by the creator and cite the source. Label contributor-supplied prompts without a public source as **source unverified**; mark unpublished prompts as **not public**. Never reverse-engineer a prompt from the result.
- Put each work in the most fitting category. Link to revised versions in the case notes.
- Link to the creator's video or live demo; do not rehost media without permission.
- Category pages group previews three per row. Landscape, square, and portrait covers have separate rows; empty cells leave room for future entries.
- A preview links to the original post; its title links to the case details. Details include the sample, public production time, implementation notes, and sourced prompts where available.
